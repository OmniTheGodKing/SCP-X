# Site-19 strategic layer: design

Status: agreed direction. Built in passes (see the end). Numbers are starting points for balancing.

## 1. Core loop

Each day:

1. Time advances: construction, research, healing, upkeep, and a chance for contained anomalies to breach.
2. Events arrive: breaches at Site-19, field incidents, Insurgency activity, O5 Council orders.
3. The player decides what to build, research and train, and which incidents to answer with which squad.
4. Missions are played on the tactical layer.
5. Results feed back: captured anomalies need cells, losses need replacing, exposure moves, research gets new material.

There should always be more problems than people, money or time, and every unanswered problem makes another one worse.

## 2. Resources

| Resource | Used for | Comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Funding | Building, research, recruiting, upkeep | Monthly O5 funding (set by standing and exposure), recontainment bonuses |
| Exposure (0–100) | Public awareness; 100 loses the campaign | Rises with unanswered incidents, failures, witnesses, anomalies at large; lowered by cover-ups |
| O5 standing | Sets funding and what the Council allows | Monthly orders, recontainments, low losses |
| D-Class | Staring, dragging, testing, cell maintenance | Monthly allotment, purchase |
| Researchers | Research and testing speed | Hired; need lab space |
| Engineers | Construction speed, cell maintenance | Hired; need workshop space |
| Power | Every facility | Generators |
| Amnestics | Cover-ups, sanity treatment | Amnestics lab, purchase |

## 3. Site-19 base

A cross-section of underground levels. Excavating deeper costs more and opens more slots. Each facility costs funding, build days, power and monthly upkeep. Placement matters: breaches spread to neighbours, and some facilities give adjacency bonuses.

| Facility | Effect |
|---|---|
| Containment wing (Safe / Euclid / Keter) | Holds anomalies; higher grade lowers breach chance |
| Security station | Lowers breach chance nearby; guards help in breaches inside the site |
| Research lab | Researcher capacity |
| Workshop | Engineer capacity; equipment production |
| Medical bay | Operatives heal twice as fast |
| Psychological services | Faster sanity recovery; treats lasting trauma traits |
| Training range | Idle operatives gain a little XP |
| Armory | Equipment storage and upgrades |
| D-Class barracks | D-Class capacity |
| Generator | Power |
| Amnestics lab | Produces amnestics |
| Communications center | Detects incidents earlier (more days to respond) |

## 4. Containment

Each contained anomaly has:

- **Upkeep:** funding, power, and sometimes something specific (SCP-173's cell must be cleaned by D-Class on a schedule, under watch; a blink during cleaning can kill).
- **Daily breach risk** from its class, the cell grade, nearby security and maintenance.
- **Testing:** spend D-Class and researcher time to fill in its file and unlock research. Tests can go wrong.
- **Transfer:** send it to another site for a one-time payment and no further risk, giving up its research.

### Breaches

A breach becomes a mission inside Site-19 on that wing's layout. Ignored or failed breaches can spread to neighbouring facilities, disabling them and killing staff until repaired.

### Escapes

- **Failed breach:** if a breach mission fails (squad lost or evacuated), the anomaly escapes Site-19.
- **Escape before arrival:** every breach also has a chance that the anomaly is already out of the site when the team arrives. It rises the longer the response takes and falls with nearby security.
- **Once out**, one of two things happens:
  - **Manhunt:** its trail is picked up and a time-limited recovery mission appears in the region where it was seen.
  - **Gone dark:** it vanishes for 10 to 30 days, then resurfaces as a field incident somewhere, often with witnesses and an exposure spike.
- **While at large:** exposure rises daily, O5 standing falls, and its cell stands empty. A failed manhunt sends it dark again.

## 5. Research

Projects cost researcher-days and funding; some need a contained anomaly or test results.

| Branch | Examples |
|---|---|
| Anomaly studies (one track per SCP) | SCP-173 movement study (shows last known location), Blink cadence protocol (lower blink chance while staring) |
| Personnel | Ocular lubricant drops (skip one blink roll per mission), Trauma counseling, Advanced firearms training |
| Equipment | Body armor, flashbangs and smoke, motion trackers, powered pallet jack (operatives drag 2 tiles) |
| Site | Containment grade upgrades, faster construction, better generators, early-warning sensors |

## 6. Personnel

Operatives pick a class at their first promotion and choose one of two perks at each rank after.

| Class | Role |
|---|---|
| Rifleman | Fighting the Insurgency: overwatch and accuracy |
| Spotter | Staring: lower blink chance, slower drain, can hand off a stare |
| Containment specialist | Drags further, seals faster, handles containment gear |
| Medic | Field healing; restores sanity to nearby allies |

- **Traits** are earned from events (*Nightmares* lowers starting sanity; *Steady eyes* lowers blink chance). Psychological services treat bad traits.
- **More task forces later:** Nu-7 "Hammer Down" (heavy combat), Eta-10 "See No Evil" (anomalies you must not look at), each with its own roster.
- **D-Class termination:** following the canon end-of-month termination keeps exposure low but loses experienced D-Class; suspending it keeps them at a small monthly exposure risk.

## 7. World map

Regions with their own exposure and a Foundation cover-up front.

- Field incidents: anomalies discovered in public, to be captured and brought to Site-19.
- Cover-up operations: agents and amnestics deal with witnesses and lower regional exposure.
- Insurgency cells: investigate to find them, then raid them.
- Other Foundation sites: requests, transfers, calls for help.

## 8. Mission types

| Mission | Where | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Containment breach | Site-19 or another site | Recontain an anomaly |
| Manhunt / field recovery | In the world | Capture an anomaly before civilians see too much |
| Insurgency raid | Site-19 | Defend the site from theft or a staged breach |
| Cover-up | In the world | Find witnesses and administer amnestics |
| Insurgency strike | An Insurgency cell | Take the fight to them |
| Escort transfer | On the road | Move an anomaly between sites under threat of ambush |

## 9. Factions and events

- **O5 Council:** monthly orders; standing sets funding; a quarterly review can end the campaign if standing collapses.
- **Chaos Insurgency:** a threat level that grows over time; steals anomalies, plants spies, ambushes transfers, sabotages facilities.
- **Ethics Committee:** audits and funding penalties for careless use of D-Class.
- **Random events:** risky test proposals, power outages that weaken containment, Insurgency defectors.

## 10. Campaign arc

- Early: SCP-173 and the Insurgency; learn the systems and build the site.
- Middle: larger Insurgency operations and pressure to expand. New anomalies are not planned yet.
- Late: find the Insurgency's main operation and take it down.
- Finale: the Insurgency's all-out assault on Site-19, aiming for a mass breach.
- Defeat: exposure reaches 100, O5 standing collapses, or Site-19 falls.
